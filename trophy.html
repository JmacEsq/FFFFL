League Lore

The Legend of The Yocum

Long before the league knew champions, punishments, or the sting of a Monday night collapse, there was only chaos — grown men arguing over waiver claims, speaking in prophecy about breakout receivers, and swearing each season would finally be “their year.”

As the story goes, the founders believed no ordinary trophy could govern such a reckless brotherhood. It needed a name with weight. A name spoken with equal parts admiration, mystery, and reverence.

And so, after many beverages and very little wisdom, the league settled upon a single sacred title:

The Yocum

Some say the name was chosen because it carried an almost mythical power over the league itself. Others claim it symbolized loyalty, legacy, and the kind of bond only lifelong friends can maintain through years of competition, betrayal, and terrible trades. A few historians insist the founders simply recognized greatness when they heard it.

What is known is this: from that day forward, the towering dark-wood trophy, crowned in bronze and etched with the names of champions past, became more than a prize. The Yocum came to represent glory, jealousy, honor, and the kind of immaturity only lifelong friends can sustain well into adulthood.

To hoist The Yocum is to etch your name into league folklore — celebrated by your brothers, resented by your rivals, and remembered long after the standings are forgotten.

Legends fade. Champions come and go. But The Yocum remains.
